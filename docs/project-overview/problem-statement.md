# Problem statement

Planning a wedding or event involves coordinating attendees, event schedules, suppliers, venue availability, budgets, invitations, and payments. When these details are scattered across spreadsheets, messages, and separate vendor systems, organizers can lose track of decisions and permissions.

UTSAV addresses this coordination problem with family workspaces and role-based collaboration, guest and RSVP management, a vendor and venue catalogue, booking and quotation workflows, budget tracking, invitations, and payment records.

## Boundaries

The application supports organizer and service-provider workflows. It is not a legal, financial, or event-safety authority. Availability, quotes, payment settlement, guest consent, and event arrangements still require user and provider confirmation. Third-party integrations and operational controls depend on deployment configuration.
